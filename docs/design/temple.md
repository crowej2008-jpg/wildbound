# SPT Temple (M18.1)

Tuning: `packages/content/tuning/temple.json` (from the owner's wiki); titles 2001–2003 give the buffs. Page
`/spt-temple`, a PvP hub card; opens with the Arena.

- Six thrones: King of Eternity (1 seat, Arena top 10, +3 % HP / P.ATK / S.ATK), Ever Victorious (2, top 20, +2 %),
  Dark Vanguard (3, top 50, +1 %). CPU trainers hold them at the start.
- Challenge: pick 1 / 5 / 10 / 100 / 500 evolutions; fight the seat's guardian at (holder's count + that). Win → the
  seat, its title (equipped if the premium slot is free) and a guardian at that count. Lose → 20 minutes' wait.
- Train: challenge your own seat; a win raises your guardian's count by the step.
- One throne at a time: taking another gives up the first (and its title).
- Each day a CPU challenger tries your seat with 50 % of the seat's CPU count + 25 per day held; if it beats your
  guardian's count, the seat (and title) is lost.
