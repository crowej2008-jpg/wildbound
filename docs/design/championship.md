# World Championship + League Championship (M18.3)

Tuning: `scripts/import-championship.mjs` → `packages/content/tuning/championship.json`. Page `/championship` (PvP
hub). Squad `championship`.

## World Championship (two-week cycle)
- Week 1, in your league (the highest league whose level your squad's best Pokémon reaches):
  - Poké Ball ladder opens Tuesday 08:00 UTC (20 floors), Great Ball Wednesday (30), Ultra Ball Friday (170).
  - Each floor is a fight against its source enemy level and pays its reward.
  - Floors cleared rank you against 60 CPU players.
- Sunday 14:00 the ladders close; the top 8 play the Master Ball knockout (14:30, 16:30, 18:30): a quarterfinal loss
  places 5th, a semifinal loss 3rd, a final loss 2nd, the winner 1st.
- From Sunday 22:00 to the end of week 2: claim the league's rank reward (1, 2, 3–8, 9–30, 31–60).

## League Championship (weekly, from server day 14)
- Qualify on Thursday by sitting in the top Ranked tier.
- From 20:00 UTC each day: Thursday round of 64 (5 battles, out at 3 losses), Friday group stage (7 battles, 5 wins to
  advance), Saturday Top 16 + quarterfinal, Sunday semifinal + final.
- Placement pays Arena Tokens: 1st 44,000, 2nd 22,000, top 4 15,000, top 8 10,000.
