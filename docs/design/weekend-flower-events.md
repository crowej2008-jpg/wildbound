# Weekend Bash + Flower Event

Tuning: `packages/content/tuning/weekend-events.json` (hand-entered from the owner's wiki; no source tables). Shown on
`/events` below the Shovel Carnival and Merge Event.

## Elven Treasure (Monday → Friday reset, UTC)
5×5 board; each flip costs 1 Elven Treasure and pays 20 Milk; the grand prize hides in one of the last 4 tiles. Finding
it pays that round's prize (rounds 1–17) and deals a new board. Buy 35 a round (10 × 24 Gems, then 25 × 35). Spending
60 / 80 / … / 600 pays cashback treasures. Milk buys boxes and shards in the Pet Exchange.

## Bingo (Friday → Monday reset)
4×4 card numbered 1–16. Each Strawberry Sauce opens a random number (repeats are wasted); ×1 or ×5. Rows pay 500
Strawberry Cake each; columns Plate Fragment ×80, Plate Bottle ×20, a Trinket Chest, Star Shards ×6,000; the two
diagonals pay a wildcard prize you pick. A finished, fully claimed card can be reset. Sauce: 40 at 20 Gems, 40 at 30
Gems a round. Cake buys from its exchange.

## Flower Event (weekly, Monday reset)
Flowers = the steps reached in this week's activity (Tower climbs, daily dungeons, adventure missions, common summons,
Pet Home merges, Misty Woods wins, Arena battles, Ranked matches, Clash of Isles battles). Seven milestone chests.
Last week's total ranks against CPU players for 5★ shards (1st also a Back Decor reward).
