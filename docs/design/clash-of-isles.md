# Clash of Isles (M14.5)

`packages/content/tuning/isles.json`, assembled from island_new_* (evening_cfg 2026-10-02) and the owner's wiki page.
Route: `/clash-of-isles` (Combat hub). Squad: `isles`. Gate: server day 2 and story 3-10. Played solo against CPU guilds.

## Built
- Weekly season Tuesday–Sunday (Monday off). 50 islands in 10 sea areas: Lesser / Medium / Greater / Premium paying
  30 / 35 / 45 / 60 Isle Tokens per 5 minutes held (source).
- Attacks cost attempts: 6 at most, +1 every 4 hours; Isle Challenge Vouchers cover the shortfall. 2 / 3 / 4 attempts at
  once add 20 / 50 / 80 % stats (source). A CPU garrison has 3 shields; each win takes one; at 0 the island is yours
  (you hold one at a time). Earlier sea areas and better tiers field stronger garrisons (stand-in).
- Holding burns development power (180 a day, 1 a minute, source); tokens are paid when collected, when you take
  another island and at the daily / weekly roll.
- Fatigue: −5 % stats per win, at most −50 %; a loss clears it (source / wiki). Every battle scores 100 season points,
  a win 100 more (source). Daily tasks at 2 / 4 / 5 challenges pay 2 / 3 / 4 Diamonds (source).
- Season rank among 1,000 CPU players; last season's rank reward is claimed the next week (Isle Tokens and 5-Star
  Pokémon Shards; Golden Egg Vouchers not copied).

## Not copied / stand-ins
CPU guilds do not raid your island; no Assist Points, cheers, shield buying or rival-island robbing; no Season Weather;
one team (the source allows 2 / 3 from world level 140 / 340). Garrison strength is a stand-in.
