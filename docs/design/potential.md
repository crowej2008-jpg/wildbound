# Potential (R4)

Copied from the source's 天赋 ("talent") system — `reference/game-rebuild/kb/potential.md` and the
`potential_cfg` dump. Generated by `scripts/import-potential.mjs` into
`packages/content/potential/potential.json`. Replaces the M7/M11 stand-in (talent "paths", five-shard
re-picks, `talents.json`).

## Who and when
- 62 Pokémon have a tree: every Legendary, Supreme, Mythic and Origin. Rare and Epic have none.
- Tier N (1–9) unlocks at 6 + N stars (7★ … 15★); the panel opens at 7★. Tiers may be unlocked in any
  order (the source client checks stars only).
- 15 distinct trees built from shared blocks: a line (A/B) fixes tiers 1, 2, 6, 8, 9; the class fixes
  tier 3; tier 5 is Embolden · Momentum · a third; tiers 4 and 7 come from six sets each.

## Costs
- Unlock = pick one of three options + the tier's fodder + Potential Shards; the option starts at Lv 1.
- Level Up to Lv 6 with Potential Shards only. Per tier: unlock 20,000 … 75,000; each level step is the
  base × 1.3 / 1.7 / 3 / 4.5 / 6.5. All nine unlocked: 465,000; all at Lv 6: 8,370,000.
- Fodder per tier and species (exact star): Legendaries/Supremes take Pokémon of their own faction
  (3★ for tiers 1–2, 5★ after); Mythics take one 5★ of each of Water / Fire / Grass, then their own copy
  from tier 5; Origins take Water / Fire / Grass 5★s, then a Light and a Dark 5★.
- **Potential Core** (`potentialCore`): stands in for one fodder Pokémon on 21 species, tiers 5–9 (15
  Mythics' own copies; the Origins' Light/Dark). No source yet (R9).
- Reset: 16 Diamonds, the whole Pokémon; everything paid comes back.

## The 67 options in battle (engine `potential/compile.ts`)
| Kind | Options | How |
|---|---|---|
| Always on | Valor, Sturdy, Natural Cure, Blessing, Loose Cannon, Immune, Rivalry, steady, Timid, Heavy Armor, Unburden, Robust, Reckless, Sniper, Tough Skin | spec `statMods` |
| Lineup | tier 1 (same-faction allies, ×2 max), tier 4 (an ally of a class) | counted when the team is built |
| Allies alive | tier 7 | buff on/off at battle start, each round start and when an ally falls |
| Events | tiers 3, 5, 9 | triggers: after an attack / normal / basic / ultimate, when hit (by a slot) |

Engine additions: `SpecInput.statMods`, `onHit` carries the attacker's skill slot (`eventSlot`
condition), `alliesAliveAtLeast` / `alliesAliveBelow` conditions, `removeBuff` effect.
