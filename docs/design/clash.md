# Cross-Server Clash (M18.2)

Tuning: `scripts/import-clash.mjs` → `packages/content/tuning/clash.json`. Page `/clash` (PvP hub). Opens on server
day 3 after story 3-10 and trainer level 10. Squad `clash` (one 6v6 team).

- **Entry:** Arena place in the top 64 when you first play that week; otherwise predictions only.
- **Tryout days (Tue / Thu / Sat):** from 19:00 UTC a 3-battle score contest (win 5, loss 2; 12+ advances and pays
  10,000 Arena Tokens, 10 5★ shards, 100,000 Gold), then knockouts at 20:05, 20:20, 20:35, 20:50, 21:05 (each win
  2,000 tokens, 10 shards, 100,000 Gold). Winning the final adds the champion bonus (20,000 / 60 / 600,000).
- **Sunday final:** for a tryout-day Top 16 (the first knockout won). Top 8, Top 4 and semifinal (5,000 / 15 /
  100,000 a win); a semifinal loss plays for third; the final pays the same again.
- Each round opens at its time and stays playable until the UTC day ends; a knockout loss ends your day.
- **Predictions:** 3,000 Prediction Coins a cup day (+ the Celebi skin perk). Back one player in each knockout round's
  featured CPU match before it starts (100–4,000 total). Right pays ×2, wrong ×0.5; claim after the round. Coins left at
  the week's end become Arena Tokens 1:1.
