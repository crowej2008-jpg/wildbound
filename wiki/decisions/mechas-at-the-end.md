# Mechas go at the very end (2026-11-04)

The R10 geared tier re-check left four of the meta six (God Ash-Pikachu, Greninja, Shiny Blastoise, Psyduck)
short of the top. Owner: *"could also be because the meta 6 rely on mechas which we dont have"*, then, on adding
them: *"lets put this at the very end when the game is done"*.

What the source has (kb/pokemon-growth.md): 9 Future War armours (Rayquaza, Groudon, Ho-Oh, Greninja,
God Ash-Pikachu, Giratina, Shiny Charizard, Shiny Blastoise, Psyduck). Each star gives its own Pokémon
+6 % Total HP / P.ATK / S.ATK (30 % at ★5) and every Pokémon +2 % HP / ATK (10 %); star skills at ★1 / ★3 / ★5;
100 upgrade levels (Lv100 ≈ HP +218k–286k, ATK +31k–66k, Total +10 %); an armoured Supreme's rarity potential
reads 17 (counts for Origin synergy and the Tower's Mythic-limit floors).

Consequence: until then the geared tier list is read without Mechas; the R10 re-check is not expected to pass
for the armoured four.
