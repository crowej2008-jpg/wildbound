# Berry Grove replaces the Island Challenge (2026-11-04)

Owner: *"for island challenge i want to swap it out for the berry grove"* — the solo berry mode he described
earlier: the main source of berries and berry materials, no new berry tier yet. Asked garden + battles or an idle
garden only, he chose **"Garden + battles"**. A brand-new mode, not a source copy (the source's closest thing is
the guild Pokémon Park boss, which pays berry materials).

- 13.8 is now [Berry Grove](../systems/berry-grove.md); the Island Challenge (90 levels + 5v5 alliance contest) is
  dropped. Same gate (story 3-10 + trainer level 10).
- Agent's design and numbers (all stand-ins, owner may retune): 6 garden plots, four seed tiers (2 / 4 / 8 / 12 h),
  a harvest = a random berry of the seed's tier + Berry Essence; 100 Grove Guardian stages (10 wins a day) paying
  seeds, Mulch and Reforge Potions on first clear; a daily Grove Boss (3 fights, cannot fall) paying seeds, Mulch,
  Essence and Reforge Potions by the share of its HP taken.
- Only the Island Challenge (source `island_travel_*`, 诸岛巡游: 90 levels + the alliance contest) is replaced.
  Clash of Isles (`island_new_*`, M14) stays planned, with the Island Shop and Tropical Shells.
