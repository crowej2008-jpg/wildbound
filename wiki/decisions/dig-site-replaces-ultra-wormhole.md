# Dig Site replaces the Ultra Wormhole (2026-11-04)

Owner: *"i really hate this mode lets make something new to replace it but still get soul hearts"*; no time gates on
getting soul hearts ("you need so many"); *"i want to stray away from just more battles"*; *"what about like the
fossil hunting that pokemon already has"*; fossils and other treasures too with soul hearts as the main reward, and
*"a museum that you use the fossils at after you cure them … when you put stuff in the museum you get stats and when
you fill groups you get even more stats"*. Walls: *"use the same system that pokemon games use"* (free, unlimited).
Curing: *"i want timers for this with a cost"*.

- 13.9 is now the [Dig Site](../systems/dig-site.md); the Ultra Wormhole (hire cards, 16 fights, weekly ranking) is
  dropped. Same gate (story 10-10 + trainer level 50).
- Agent's numbers (stand-ins): 13 × 10 wall, rock 2–6 deep, stability 100 (pick 3, hammer 6), 2–4 items and 2–5
  bedrock pieces a wall; soul hearts weighted blue 28 / purple 17 / orange 8 / red 3 with bigger shapes for better
  ones; 3 lab slots, cures 2 h / 100,000 Gold to 8 h / 500,000 Gold; 26 museum pieces in 8 groups; spares → Soul-heart EXP.
- Owner (later the same night): *"i want the museum groups to give stats that are rarer instead of just hp and atk"* —
  each full group now adds one rarer line (Block, Guard Break, DMG Reduction, DMG Boost, Crit Intensity, Control
  Boost, Crit Resistance, Crit Chance; 1–4 %, agent's numbers). Pieces still give HP / ATK.
- Owner: *"i want it to be a big part of power, because i want berries and most items to go into the muesem too and
  each group gives a couple different stats and always flat hp and atks and %hp and atks"*; donated items are used up
  ("It's used up"); kinds: berries, held items, orbs, soul hearts; *"i want players to choose what to put not it auto
  goes there"*. Built: 234 spots in 31 groups (`scripts/generate-museum.mjs`), agent's numbers.
