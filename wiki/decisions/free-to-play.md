# Free to play, no real money (2026-10-23)

Owner: *"anything that costs real money we will remove for now, i want the game to be almost
completely free to play for me and my friends."*

**Removed (not simulated):** real-money top-ups, purchase buttons, paid Vault passes, Pet Home paid
assistant, premium/paid event tracks, paid dice, paid-only conveniences, Great Value Investment.

**Kept:** Gems as an **earnable** currency. Any gem-priced reset/convenience must have an earnable
path. "Premium" tickets can exist as rewards. VIP is driven by lifetime XP, not purchases.

Side effect: the chapter-complete mail now pays a flat **200 gems** (the old `100 + 50 × chapter`
formula would pay 9,200 at chapter 182).
