# Rift Arena: a purpose for the Advanced track (2026-11-04)

Owner: *"i want a purpose for rift because its pretty useless already"*, then, on the agent's options (a Merchant
selling something scarce + milestone rewards up the Advanced track): *"ya lets do that its the second half of rift
that is super useless"*. A deliberate departure from the source (which drops only Time Coins on floors 101–250).

- **Advanced milestones** every 25 floors (125 … 250): Premium tickets, Resetter Shards, Diamonds, a red Orb +3,
  a random Mythic held item (agent's numbers, owner may retune).
- **Mystic Merchant** sells scarce goods for Time Coins with per-season limits (Resetter / Potential / Held Item
  Shards, Premium tickets, one Mythic held item a season), plus the source's six orb-box rows for Diamonds from
  floor 41. A slot opens at each milestone floor (the source's rule).
