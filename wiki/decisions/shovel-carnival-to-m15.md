# Shovel Carnival moves to M15 (2026-11-05)

The source's Shovel Carnival is a limited-time event (4 days every 2 weeks): a Diamond-pool prize wheel spun with
Treasure Shovels (bought with Diamonds), Credit milestones, a Treasure Piece exchange and a Mecha shop. Owner:
*"leave it with m15"* — it is built with the other events (M15), not in M14. The Mecha shop waits for Mechas (not on the
roadmap).
