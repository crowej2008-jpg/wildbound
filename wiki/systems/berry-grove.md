# Berry Grove (M13.8) — gate: clear 3-10 + Lv10

The owner's replacement for the Island Challenge ([decision](../decisions/berry-grove-replaces-island.md)). Solo;
the main source of berries and berry materials.

- **Garden:** 6 plots. Plant a seed (Common / Basic / Intermediate / Greater: 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 h); harvest = one
  random berry of that tier + 50 / 100 / 200 / 400 Berry Essence. Harvest one plot or everything ripe.
- **Grove Guardians:** 100 stages, 10 wins a day (losses free); first clears pay seeds, Mulch (blue → red by band of
  25) and Reforge Potions; every 10th stage adds a Greater seed and 500 Essence.
- **Grove Boss:** one per weekday (Celebi, Shaymin, Shiny Venusaur, Zygarde, Rayquaza, Dialga, Snorlax), 3 fights a
  day; it cannot fall; pay = its band's full pay × the share of its HP taken (at least 20 %). Its level follows
  the next Guardian stage.
- Squad `berryGrove`; save v44. Design: `docs/design/berry-grove.md`; ASSUMPTIONS #104.
