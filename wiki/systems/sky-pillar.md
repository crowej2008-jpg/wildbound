# Sky Pillar (M13.2) — gate: clear 7-1

- **180 floors × 10 stages** in six 30-floor wings (1,800 stages), generated by
  `scripts/generate-sky-pillar.mjs` → `tuning/sky-pillar.json`.
- Up to 3★ per stage: the clear + two of "knockouts ≤ N", "every survivor above N% HP", "rounds ≤ N".
- Floor chests at 10 / 20 / 30 stars.
- **Proofs of Sky:** cap 6, one recharges every 8 hours (the cap stops the clock, not the purse).
  First clear costs a proof; re-challenging a passed stage is free; a sweep costs a proof and pays half
  the highest 3★ record's reward.
- 15-turn counter is a **loss on time-out** (`BattleRules.roundLimit`, Sky Pillar only).
- Floor level gates follow the source but cap at **80** (our trainer cap) instead of 200.
- `skyPillar` squad mode; save v17; route `/explore/sky-pillar`.
- Gate: 510 unit / 90 e2e green at completion.
