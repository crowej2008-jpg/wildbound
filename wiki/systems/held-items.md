# Held Items

(Code id: **relics**.)

## Built (R5, 2026-10-31) — the source's system
Code: `packages/engine/src/equipment/held.ts` (rules), `server/src/equipment.ts` (actions),
`server/src/equipment-view.ts` (`RelicView`), client `pages/wild/HeldItemPanel.tsx`. Data:
`content/equipment/held-items.json`, generated by `scripts/import-held-items.mjs` (re-run it on the
owner's PC with `WILDBOUND_SOURCE`); hand-written battle effects in `scripts/data/held-item-effects.json`.
- **75 items**, Rare 9 · Epic 9 · Legendary 12 · Supreme 18 · Mythic 27; class-locked; worn by
  Supreme / Mythic / Origin only (Origin was blocked before R5 — fixed).
- **Rolled at level 0:** main-stat group (HP / P.ATK / S.ATK, a third each, any class) — flat value +
  `stren_value` per level, and from Legendary its Total % (+ every 5 levels); plus 1/2/3/5/6 lines
  from the quality's 31-line pool, equal odds, uniform value. Lines never grow.
- **In battle:** faction lines (20 of 31) count only on that faction; Absorb HP Rate → lifesteal;
  Crit INT → `critPower`, Block INT → `blockPower`, treatment lines → heal boosts.
- **Levels** 0–200, `held_item_upgrade_json` (7,350 Held Item Shards); Lv 31/61/81/101/121/141/161/181
  need Link Crystal level 250 … 600 (kb, not dumped).
- **Refine** (worn items only): roll one line slot → pending → **Keep / Drop**. Supreme 2 / Mythic 3
  slots may ever be refined; the main stat never. One pending roll at a time.
  Price: **owner's ladder** in Held Item Resetters — free, 1, 1, 2, 2 … 10, 10 (21 refines, 110).
- **Lock** toggles; **dismantle** (unworn, unlocked) pays 5/8/10/15/20 shards + all level shards.
- **Resetters:** 20 Resetter Shards → 1 Held Item Resetter (button on the panel).
- **Skills:** source text on every Legendary+ item; **active in battle:** Choice Specs (round 4
  damage), Razor Fang (+speed rounds 3–5), Razor Claw (Guard Break on even rounds). The other 24
  names show "not active in battle yet" — see [open questions](../open-questions.md).
- **Not built:** the album (tables not dumped); drops come with [Hoopa's Vault](hoopa-vault.md) (13.3).
  New accounts start with three Rares (one per class) as a stand-in.

## How the source game refines (found 2026-10-31 in `reference/game-rebuild/kb/held-items.md`)
**Refine in the source is a re-roll, not a power-up.**
- Items carry **random extra lines** by quality: Rare 1 · Epic 2 · Legendary 3 · Supreme 5 · Mythic 6,
  drawn at equal odds from a pool of 31 (faction Ult/Skill DMG up/RED, Crit, Crit Res, Crit INT,
  Block, Block INT, Guard Break, Control Boost/Res, Absorb, two healing lines).
- A refine re-rolls **one** line into a brand-new line + value; the player **keeps or drops** it.
- Only some slots can ever be refined: Mythic 3, Supreme 2, lower 0. The main stat never.
- Cost in Held Item Resetters by refine count: 1,1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,12,14,16,18,20, then 20.
- The item's special effect does **not** grow with refines.

The M7/M11 refine (+20% to the item's effect) was an invention; R5 replaced it with this model.
