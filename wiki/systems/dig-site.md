# Dig Site (M13.9) — gate: clear 10-10 + Lv50

The owner's replacement for the Ultra Wormhole ([decision](../decisions/dig-site-replaces-ultra-wormhole.md)). No
battles; the main source of soul hearts.

- **Walls** (free, unlimited — the main series' fossil-digging rules): a 13 × 10 grid of rock 2–6 deep with bedrock
  and 2–4 buried items. The **pick** wears 2 at the spot and 1 around it (shake 3); the **hammer** wears 2 at the
  spot, 2 on the sides and 1 on the corners (shake 6). At 100 shake the wall collapses; only fully uncovered items
  are kept. "Stop digging" keeps what is free.
- **Finds:** soul hearts (blue / purple / orange / red; a random set and socket), Soul Stones (200 / 800
  Soul-heart EXP), 11 fossils, 15 treasures.
- **Lab:** 3 slots; a fossil cures for 2–8 h and 100,000–500,000 Gold.
- **Museum** (a big part of power): 234 spots in 31 groups — fossils and treasures, every berry, held item, orb
  (kind × colour) and soul heart (set × socket). The player picks what to donate; the copy is used up. Each spot gives
  flat HP / ATK; a full group gives flat HP / ATK, % HP / ATK and two rarer stats. Spare pieces turn into Soul-heart EXP.
