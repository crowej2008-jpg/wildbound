# Faction Tower (M13.7) — gate: clear 28-10

- **3,000 floors** (source count, per 6B). Survey said three towers mapped to factions with a weekday
  rotation; 20 climbs/day; 2 free sweeps/day + bought sweeps.
- Floor reward curve; achievement rewards every 5 floors; leaderboard.
- Faction restriction; two-team floors past a threshold.
- Shop: trinket chests, Star Shards, gold; daily restock. Weekly reset.

Different from the M8 [Tower](pve-modes.md) and the [co-op boss tower idea](../ideas/coop-boss-tower.md).
